The “tagged as” column exposes that disagreement.

  • Parser: a day-first tenant’s date reaches the shared month-first parser and its day and month are exchanged. This includes app and email reports, and form reports from booking-v2. For example, R-41200 reports 7 April becoming 4 July.
  • Hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY for day-first tenants. People follow the hint, but the form reads the value in the tenant’s date order. This affects form reports from Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour. R-41230 belongs here even though intake called it parser. R-41205 belongs to parser even though intake called it hint.

Fix the parser to respect the input’s declared date order. Separately, make booking-v3’s hint agree with its actual locale-aware input handling. Test those paths separately; matching symptoms do not prove the same fix.
